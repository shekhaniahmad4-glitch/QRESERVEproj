Help & Support

Frequently asked questions and direct campus contact information.

  How do I request an official document?

Requesting an official document takes just a few simple steps. Follow these:

1
Go to the "My Requests" tab
Click "My Requests" on the left sidebar. You'll see all the available document types on this page.
2
Choose your document
Click the type you need — like Transcript of Records, Report of Grades, Certificate of Registration, or Good Moral.
3
Click "Request now"
Press the "Request now" button below the document cards. Your request is submitted instantly.
4
Get your queue ticket
You'll be taken to "My Queue" where your ticket (queue number, counter, and QR code) is shown. Download the QR code and bring it when picking up your document.
  What should I bring when picking up documents?
Please bring your digital QR Code (downloadable from My Queue) along with your official BulSU Student ID or valid photo ID when your queue number is called.
  What are the registrar office hours?
The Registrar and Finance offices operate Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (excluding official holidays).
