Help & Support

Frequently asked questions and contact directories for campus offices.

How does the QRESERVE queue system work?
After reserving a queue slot, your ticket number appears on your dashboard. When your number is called, the live monitor updates with the assigned counter. Please proceed to the window promptly.
How do I update my profile details and photo?
Navigate to the Profile tab in the sidebar panel. You can enter your full name, age, course/program, year level, section, and upload a custom profile picture (JPG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP).
Can former students or guests request credentials?
Yes! Alumni, graduates, and transferees can log in through the Guest portal, select their category, and view queue monitors and campus office updates.
What should I do if I miss my turn at the counter?
Tickets that are missed will be placed on brief hold for 10 minutes. If unclaimed, you may easily request a new queue number through your dashboard.
